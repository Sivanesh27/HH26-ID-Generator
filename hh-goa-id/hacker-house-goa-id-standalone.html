The Model

A rolling challenge,
not a waiting list.

Everyone builds. No one is just watching.

Instead of filling one application and waiting for the result, you earn your spot by completing different skill-based tasks. New tasks open one after another. If you miss one, you can still try the next. Keep participating until your whole team is in.

Stage One · August

Open Trials

Anyone can join. These tasks are based only on your skills and performance — no partner, no gatekeeping.

Stage Two · September

Partner Trials

Run with our partners. They look for builders who match their goals and needs — and it's a fresh door for anyone not yet in.

Getting selected in a trial does not guarantee your seat. Your seat is confirmed only after the RSVP & Stake process in late September, when every member of your team confirms.

How a team clears an Open Trial

  1. Teams of one to three. The team is the unit of selection. Solo builders are welcome too.
  2. Everyone submits. Each member completes and submits the same task on their own.
  3. All or nothing. A team is selected only when every member has submitted and passed.
  4. Try as often as you like. Missed a task? Try the next one. Keep going until you're in.
Every selected builder, not just team leads, has personally cleared a task. Quality is set at the door.

Partner Trials, in short

  • Their own deadlines. Each Partner Trial runs on its own timeline.
  • The same standard. Every member still submits their own task.
  • A second door. Builders not yet selected in Open Trials get a fresh way in.

What We Look For

Criteria & your seat

Selection is based on merit across both stages. How you perform on the task is the main signal — the rest adds context.

01

Proof of building

Shipped projects, repositories, hackathon results. What you have built matters more than what you say.

02

Task performance

How you actually solve the task. This is the main signal.

03

Clear thinking

The ability to reason about problems worth solving. You do not need a finished idea to apply.

04

Drive to be there

A real wish to take part. With limited seats, this makes a difference.

RSVP & Stake

Being selected does not confirm your seat. In the last week of September, selected builders confirm by staking. A team's seat is locked only once every member has staked.

Seats & the hybrid option

The residency is built for 247 in-person builders. That number can grow with demand. Where it fits, some tracks may open to remote builders.
